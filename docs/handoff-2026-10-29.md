Available: `openai/gpt-oss-120b`, `gpt-oss-20b`, `qwen/qwen3.8-27b`. gpt-oss-120b answers in ~4 s with valid JSON, but the free **tokens-per-minute cap** returned 429 on 11 of 13 calls (Retry-After 7–37 s).
