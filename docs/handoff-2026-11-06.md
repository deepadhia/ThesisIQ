## Guidance pacing + fulfilment record (user: "stock prices move on guidance and fulfilment, which we track in the db; important for management credibility") — uncommitted; lint clean, `test:heart` 421, `npm test` 46 files / **851 tests**
**What was missing:** the parser threw away the columns a Q2 / Q3 statement already prints (half-year / nine-month, the same period last year, last year's full year), so only the quarter's growth could be compared with a full-year guide.
* **Parser** (`financial-validator.service.js`): reads the cumulative columns only when the revenue row proves the layout (H1 = the two quarters; year-ago part <= year-ago whole; never guessed). Six columns (Sep / Dec) give `ytd*` and `fyPrior*`; five columns give `ytd*` without a full-year base (TRANSRAIL); a five-column MARCH statement gives the FULL YEAR just ended (`fy*`, `fyEnded`). `periodEndHint` (from the filing date, `quarterEndBefore`) unlocks it when header dates are unreadable (SHAKTIPUMP's real PDF). Hand-verified against the printed rows of the real Shakti page (H1 revenue 746.03 + 858.67 = 1,604.70; EBITDA 146.89).
* **Pacing engine** (`guidance-pacing.service.js`): per full-year guide (revenue growth %, revenue ₹, profit growth, EBITDA margin): the year to date vs the guide and what the rest of the year must deliver (e.g. guide 35-40%, H1 +24.5%: "H2 needs +44.6%": BEHIND). Statuses AHEAD / ON TRACK / STRETCH / BEHIND (gap thresholds -5 / 3 / 10 pts, defaults in `PACING`); when the filing prints no last-year base it says "run-rate" and never claims what H2 must deliver. At year end (March statement) the same guide is JUDGED: MET / PARTIAL / MISSED for BOTH the ORIGINAL guide (the credibility test) and the guide in force after revisions. Guards found by running on the real database (each with a test using the real sentence): multi-year guides ("next two years"), segment / brand growth, first-half or quarterly figures, margins several times off the company's own, margin metrics whose statement is not about a margin, ₹ targets below the year to date or far outside last year's revenue.
* **Database** (migration `supabase/migrations/20261107000000_guidance_tracking.sql`, NOT yet applied): `guidance_pacing_history` (each quarter's reading per guide) and `guidance_outcomes` (verified year-end outcomes). The worker upserts both (idempotent) and shows pacing, the year-end outcome and the verified TRACK RECORD (original guides, misses first) in the flash and the digest. A missing table never blocks an alert (warning); the self-audit reports `GUIDANCE_TABLES_MISSING`. **Run `npm run db:migrate` (additive, CREATE TABLE IF NOT EXISTS) before relying on history.**
* **Allocation gate:** a pacing status that worsens to STRETCH / BEHIND, recovers from BEHIND, or is first read as BEHIND is a push reason ("guidance pacing: Revenue growth FY27 guide 13–14% growth now BEHIND (was ON TRACK)"). The previous view carries the last pacing reading.
* **Why `management_commitments` is not used for credibility:** it holds 4,775 commitments, 3,197 still "Pending" and 1,506 "Achieved" against 66 "Missed" (96%), with hundreds of repeated extractions per stock (GRAVITA 558, ELECON 511). Loose AI labels inflate it. Credibility here rests on verified statement numbers.
* **Live checks (read-only):** pacing on the real Q2 statements and stored guidance: ANANTRAJ guide 15% vs H1 +5.7% BEHIND (H2 needs +23.8%); ELECON guide ~15% vs H1 +8.5% BEHIND; CCL STRETCH; LUMAXTECH AHEAD; TRANSRAIL STRETCH (run-rate). Backfill dry run (`backfill-guidance-outcomes.js`, writes nothing without `--apply`): FY26 outcomes for 3 stocks that parsed: GRAVITA revenue guide 20–30% delivered +10.3% MISSED and EBITDA margin >12% delivered 10.2% MISSED; JSLL margin >30% delivered 47.4% MET and revenue ₹650–700 Cr delivered ₹801 Cr MET.
* **Limits (say so):** margin verdicts use OUR EBITDA (excludes other income) and say so on the line; a company defining it differently can read differently. Of 18 March-quarter filings only 3 produced year-end outcomes (others: statement unreadable, no full-year column in that layout, or an unreadable PDF): the track record will be thin until more layouts are handled. Guide extraction comes from the AI-filled `management_commitments`; each pacing line is guarded and the source statement is stored with it, but it can still mis-read a sentence. First-reading BEHIND will push once per stock after deploy.
